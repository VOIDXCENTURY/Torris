OUR STORY

Tailored for those who refuse to blend in.

TORRIS was founded on a single idea: the modern man deserves clothing built with the same precision as fine architecture. Every shirt begins as raw premium cotton and Belgian linen, cut by hand and finished with reinforced stitching that outlasts trend cycles.

From our atelier to your wardrobe, we obsess over the details so you don't have to — silhouette, drape, breathability, and a finish that photographs as good as it feels.
